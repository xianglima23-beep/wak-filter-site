Frequently Asked Questions

Automotive Filter Wholesale FAQ

Common questions about MOQ, samples, customization, production and quotations.

What is your minimum order quantity?

Our standard MOQ starts from 100 pcs, depending on the filter model and packaging requirements.

Can I request samples before placing a bulk order?

Yes. Samples are available for quality and fitment checking before bulk production.

Do you support OEM and private-label packaging?

Yes. We support OEM/ODM services, custom labels, boxes and packaging for qualified orders.

How long does production take?

Sample preparation usually takes 3–7 days. Bulk production time depends on the model and order quantity.

What information should I send for a quotation?

Please send the OEM number, vehicle model, required quantity and a product photo if available.

Can you help identify a filter by OE number?

Yes. Send us the OE or OEM reference number, vehicle application or product photo, and our team will check available matching filter references.

Do you support international shipping?

Yes. We serve customers in 50+ countries and support international shipping arrangements based on destination, order volume and buyer requirements.
